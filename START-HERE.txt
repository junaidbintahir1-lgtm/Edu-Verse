EDUVERSE — NEW ORBIT DESIGN
===========================

1. Unzip this folder.
2. Double-click index.html. That's the new black 3D orbit design.
3. Sign in with your EduVerse account (or create one on the sign-in page).

What's in here:
- index.html, core.js, views.js, views2.js, menu.js, live.js : the new design
- data/snapshot.js : your subjects, notes, flashcards and questions (a copy of the live site's data)
- resources/, notes/ : the PDFs
- database-update.sql : run once in Supabase (SQL Editor) to switch on effort-only XP, seasons and the leaderboard choice
- HANDOFF.md : how everything works

Note: this copy carries a snapshot of your data. The version on GitHub
(branch claude/new-design, folder new-design/) reads the live index.html directly instead.
